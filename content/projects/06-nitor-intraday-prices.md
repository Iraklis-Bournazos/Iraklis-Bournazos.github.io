Participated in Nitor Energy’s 2026 Quantitative Trading Case Competition, developing machine-learning models to forecast intraday electricity prices from weather and energy-market features.


The final model combined extensive feature engineering with gradient-boosted models such as LightGBM and CatBoost, with particular focus on handling the strongly heavy-tailed price distribution and rare price spikes.
